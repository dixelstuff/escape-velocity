## Repetition

The new twenty has 60 word positions and 57 unique spelling identities. However:

- 40/60 positions appeared somewhere in the earlier permanent archive.
- 28/60 appeared in its previous 150 definitions.

- By role, earlier-archive reuse is 13/20 tees, 16/20 doglegs and 11/20 flags.

Counts include historical withdrawn/retired definitions; they are not claims about exactly which words this player saw. The concentration of familiar social/abstract doglegs nonetheless supports the complaint. HONOUR, JUSTICE, CURTAIN, PATIENCE, DUTY, PROMISE and RESCUE recur especially often. The repeated nomination pool and existing context graph favoured already well-covered words. Internal within-batch uniqueness missed that cross-batch staleness.

## Selection changes

1. Preserve an inviting opening: inspect ordinary first thoughts from several senses and distinguish strong tee links rejected by safety from weak contact. Evaluate understandable continuations from small setup shots.
2. Inspect root contexts behind reached landing counts; do not count many descendants of one opening as diverse access.
3. Draw fresh familiar vocabulary from broad independent domains before using the existing graph; add new independent contexts rather than repeatedly selecting graph-covered favourites.
4. Audit recent repetition, especially doglegs, and document deliberate repeats. Avoid another roster dominated by virtues/emotions without banning abstractions or forcing quirky/rare words.
5. Keep the one-third welcoming aim and meaningful second-leg choices. Do not equate a frustrating opening with a satisfying discovery hole.

I recommend replacing/avoiding this tee–dogleg pairing in the next authorised revision. This audit does not silently swap the current hole or invalidate any progress.
