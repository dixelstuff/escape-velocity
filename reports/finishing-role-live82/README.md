## Local records and Your game

Old active Cleverness numbers are removed from durable best summaries on load and merge. Route words, saved engine states, fewest strokes, attempts, completion/recency dates, custom definitions and historical research reports remain. The old route/version identity remains available for lazy reanalysis.

Only retained routes of the visited hole are sent for fresh vector scoring. No mass startup replay occurs. A hole enters **all** Your game counts, lists and averages only when its fewest-strokes route has a finite v9 score. Result-category averages use that route, while Overall Cleverness uses the highest current-version score available for the hole. A pending old higher-Cleverness route cannot hide a newly calculated stroke-result route. Old reports are excluded and same-release stale merges cannot resurrect invalidated scores. Separate export-reset and batch protections are unchanged.
