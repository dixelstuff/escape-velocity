## Interface decisions

Show best score alongside the earliest attempt achieving it; later unsuccessful play should not change that milestone. Log headings report attempts still available, including an unfinished current attempt. Results-embedded logs remain open until their Hide attempt log link is used; during-play logs still dismiss when tapping the map.

## Proposed scoring-limit behaviour (not implemented)

After the final scored attempt, show a compact score-lock dialog: “Score locked: Birdie (−1) · Best on attempt 9”. Offer Next hole and Keep practising. Practice continues from any reached lie with normal shot feedback and unlimited unranked retries, preserving the scored result and earliest-best attempt. Store practice separately; exclude it from course ranking. Rank equal scores by earliest-best attempt, not total subsequent exploration. Review this policy before changing score-limit state handling.
