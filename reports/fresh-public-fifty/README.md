# Fresh public fifty — 4 November 2026

The player rejected the deliberate quirky/playful vocabulary direction. This collection returns to the previously approved open-landscape principles: familiar, versatile words; natural openings; credible actual dogleg handoffs; varied concrete and abstract contexts; and no authored intended solution. Word variety is welcome without a novelty, rarity or surprise quota.

## Public and admin

These are **fifty genuinely new combinations**, replacing the entire previous public selection as **#1–#50**. No prior public ID is reused. Admin retains all previous holes and progress, and adds this batch as **#432–#481**. Public withdrawal is not global retirement. Custom holes, best routes and export history are not deleted, copied to new IDs or renumbered internally.

The public manifest contains only this batch. Its first hole is SUPPORT → WEATHER → PEN. Existing saved public selections outside the batch fall back to this hole without erasing their archived records. Existing results remain available via `?admin=1`. Public stats are scoped to the new collection, as before.

## Selection process and limits

- Fixed 160 word-only nominations before inspecting complete routes. General association notes were written per word, independently of a candidate's eventual route.
- Two nominations containing MARK were excluded because this model's supported vocabulary does not contain it. No vocabulary/model change was made.
- Screened the remaining 158 with the existing authoritative rules. Directly accessible legs were rejected using the unchanged direct-leg check.
- Reviewed 78 candidates more deeply from actual reached landing states, including their prefixes, baselines, automatic finishes and a shared onward-word pool.
- Selected fifty after reviewing the combination, opening contexts, meaning of the named dogleg and possible handoffs. Safety examples remain offline evidence, never intended routes in live definitions. No Cleverness scores, shortest-route objectives, fixed grammatical pattern, minimum difficulty or simulated performance target were used.

SETTLE → MUSIC → SHELL was set aside because its inspected examples leaned on weak book/music or space/music contexts and repeated COVER. COUNT → SHADOW → GARDEN showed a narrower opening sample followed by many immediate finishes. INSIDE → FORTUNE → FEATHER had a diffuse hero/legend/fame approach. These are editorial concerns, not proof that no good human route exists. Missing graph context is uncertain, not an impossible association.

The first holes favour independently familiar opening contexts rather than deliberately remote playful/abstract pairings. The whole set mixes actions, qualities, objects and social ideas. There are **118 distinct words across 150 slots**, and fifty distinct tee words. Familiar words may recur in different combinations; novelty never outweighs usability.

No bounded graph check can certify enjoyment or predict a person's stroke count. A short example is not automatically trivial, a long one is not automatically bad, and numerous sampled landings do not establish human variety. This is a fresh human playtest under the returned editorial direction.

## Validation

All fifty complete safety examples replayed against the authoritative engine. Tests independently restore **2,116 reached dogleg lies** and replay **15,842 deterministic samples of onward shots**, plus the available short-finish examples. Full offline evidence is in `safety.json`; it is bounded rather than exhaustive.

Tests verify that all previous 460 definitions remain structurally identical, new IDs/dates are unique, public membership is exactly the new fifty, prior public/archived/custom progress remains intact, and public/admin results stay correctly scoped. All holes remain mandatory-dogleg Par 5 with unlimited exploration. Gameplay, scoring, automatic finishing, thresholds and vectors are unchanged.

`candidates.json`, `preflight.json`, `initial-review.json`, `review-candidates.json` and `candidate-review.json` preserve the nomination/review record. `proposals.json` contains words and independent contexts, while `validation.json` records the original archive hash and added definitions. `scripts/append_fresh_public_fifty.mjs` publishes the selection.
