## Editorial direction

A small discovery before the dogleg, rather than simply entering a tee's obvious category. Keep familiar words and understandable associations; vary changes of sense with materials, functions and physical connections. Include a few welcoming discoveries, a majority of moderate setup journeys, and several deeper possibilities. Quirkiness should come from a recognisable turn, not an unexplained low-similarity bridge.

The examples include tools changing into ear anatomy, trailers changing into films, mint changing from coins to herbs, and a slide changing from an instrument to a chest of treasure. These explanations are qualitative editorial checks, not evidence that difficulty or human satisfaction has been measured.

All 50 examples complete using the authoritative engine with spelling equivalence, plural/family rescue, direction, current dogleg tolerance and subsequent second-leg finishing allowance. Both literal direct legs are rejected on all 50. **45/50** verification examples take at least two shots to reach the dogleg. This is a route audit, not a shortest-solution search: alternative landings and quicker routes remain possible and welcome. Verified examples range from 3 to 6 strokes.

There are 133 endpoint words absent from the earlier published/local bank; some familiar endpoint words are deliberately reused in different journeys. No complete three-word hole is repeated. Normal seeded map variation and alternating dogleg directions are retained. No gameplay rules, Cleverness formula, thresholds, vectors, attempt limits or UI layout changed.
