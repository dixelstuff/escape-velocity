## Refined selection/checking pipeline

1. Nominate familiar words and combinations independently of any intended route. Expand the pool across everyday objects, physical actions, emotions, social situations, properties and different senses. Do not compose a route and then choose a dogleg to fit it.
2. Prefer a more substantial second-leg landscape without maximising vector distance or making every hole hard. Word quality, natural relationships and room to discover remain editorial judgements.
3. Check the named-word direct-leg rule with the unchanged engine. Reach alternative dogleg words through ordinary context associations, then preserve each actual lie, prefix, baseline and leg restrictions.
4. Probe **the same union of candidate next words from every reached lie**. Record contact/progress/finishing differences, not merely each lie's different hand-picked neighbour list. Also record natural supported options, automatic finishes on arrival, explicit shots at the flag, and completions within the next one or two player inputs. Automatic finishing strokes are counted by the engine.
5. Review obvious shared-bridge shortcuts individually, including any early arrival finishes. A low overall shortcut share can still hide one obvious LIGHT-like answer. In the selected sample there are no one- or two-input arrival finishes; a few longer approaches still finish on arrival. This is observed coverage, not proof no shorter route exists.
6. Critically review weak combinations before publication. A legal completion is not enough. Reject sampled arbitrary associations, sparse openings/landings, unconvincing combinations and overused words; do not lengthen routes for their own sake. Legal witness paths remain diagnostic spoilers, never live hints or intended solutions.

The checker remains a **bounded editorial aid**, not a natural-language oracle or automated fun classifier. Its independent per-word context notes can miss legitimate senses and contain generic associations. All suggested shots are engine-validated; every accepted completion is independently replayed. Model validity alone never settles human credibility. No phrase resource, lexical asset, scoring change or new browser dependency was introduced.

## Vocabulary and dogleg evidence

- **90 distinct words across the new 30; 81 absent from the prior schedule**, counting curated UK/US forms as equivalent. None uses AMBITION, COMFORT, GRAVITY, HONOUR or RIBBON.
- Named dogleg/flag median cosine similarity is **0.014**, versus **0.087** in the preceding 30. Means are 0.006 versus 0.090. Lower similarity means greater vector separation; it is not a guarantee of better or harder gameplay and no global cutoff was added.
- **556 reached landing words** across the 30 checks, at least three per hole. All holes show at least two distinct mechanical onward-option sets and two natural-option sets from a shared probe pool.
- Arrival automatic finishes: **6/556** sampled landings. Natural completion with at most one subsequent chosen word: **17/556**, including those arrival finishes. Each hole is below 50% on both measures; most show none. These are context-graph sample shares, not probabilities for player choices or exhaustive vocabulary proportions.
- Probe limits: five approach inputs, seven subsequent inputs, beam 160, up to 64 distinct reached landings. The short-finish probe checks two player inputs through the local context graph. A missing short finish means **not found here**, not impossible or objectively hard.
- Context graph: 1874 supported words, shared across the 90 candidate triples. Five unsupported note words (CRUNCHY, GOOSEBUMPS, MARK, PUNCHLINE, SEEDS) were excluded; none is a hole word. The game vocabulary/vectors are unchanged.

### Inspecting a genuine landing choice (spoilers)

On RUMOUR → SHELF → THIRST, sampled SEA has a supported WATER finish (two remaining strokes including the automatic flag). Literal SHELF instead offers BOOK/CUPBOARD/DISPLAY/STORAGE, with no finish found within the two-input probe. SEA is a familiar continental-shelf sense; SHELF also supports storage/display senses. These were discovered after fixing the triple, not used to author it.

On CARELESS → BANNER → MURMUR, PROTEST has a supported VOICE finish (two remaining strokes). CLOTH has SOFT → WHISPER (three remaining strokes including the flag); PAPER has other onward options, with no two-input finish found. The same target and thresholds produce genuinely different choices from these reached lies.

### Feedback controls and limitations

The [control audit](controls-audit.json) runs the same checks on the seven removals and four liked holes. TIDE's high sampled immediate-finish share supports its shortcut concern, but SLEEP's obvious LIGHT route accounts for only a small share of sampled landings. Conversely, the liked SPARE hole has a high sampled arrival-finish share and its context-graph completion check is incomplete. These controls show that neither a share cutoff nor search success can reproduce enjoyment. Do not tune notes to make the labels agree or use the screen to retire additional holes automatically.

## Critical review before finalising

Ninety word-only triples were considered; 60 were not published. The [candidate review](candidate-review.json) records each disposition. Examples:

- AWAKE → FRACTION → PLEAD: rejected despite completion evidence; the sampled VISIBLE/FRACTION handoff is hard to justify naturally.
- SNAP → VOW → CURIOUS: rejected despite a completion; familiar PROMPT/READY links and a relatively high arrival-finish share risk a shallow shortcut.
- SCATTER → APPETITE → WITNESS: rejected despite a completion; the sampled bodily/category chain is diffuse and unpersuasive.
- DELAY → FROTH → TRUST: only one reached landing in these bounds; insufficient demonstrated handoff variety.
- Several others fail the existing direct-leg rule or have no supported completion in the bounded check. That is insufficient evidence for this batch, not a claim that a player cannot solve them.
- Other mechanically acceptable candidates were passed over to keep the final bank varied, including concrete tees with multiple meanings. No word is automatically rejected just for being a noun, animal, food, common or a literal dogleg.

The final batch is a carefully screened playtest selection, not a claim that arithmetic establishes excellence. Human feedback should decide what survives.
