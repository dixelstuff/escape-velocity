# Rebalanced public fifty — 3 November 2026

The public collection remains **50 holes: 20 playtested holes from the previous thirty, mixed with 30 of the newer playful batch**. All 431 admin holes remain available. Nothing is retired globally and no stored record is erased or reassigned.

The player found the first few new holes hard. The playful vocabulary had often been paired with remote abstract doglegs. Legal completions and numerous sampled landings were insufficient evidence of welcoming natural access. Replacing the early run is a response to that human feedback, not a claim that every individual removed combination has been proved bad.

The first two are STRIKE → KINDNESS → CURTAIN and WINK → SEASON → GRIP. These took eight and four fresh word submissions respectively through the player's first captured finish. Proven holes recur throughout, including a couple of satisfying quick eagles (CLIP and TWIST), rather than putting all easier material first or maximising difficulty later. The prior playtested selection ranges from two to twenty-three submitted words before its first captured finish; effort is context, not a quality or difficulty label.

Twenty newer combinations leave the public view. Concerns include indirect physical-to-abstract approaches, repetitive/generic safety examples and a few narrower sampled landing choices. No minimum route length, arbitrary landing quota or vector threshold has been introduced. Retained quirky holes include GIGGLE, CONFETTI, DAZZLE, CHUCKLE, GRUMBLE, FLUTTER, SPARKLE, FLUFFY and PICKLE. These remain human playtests, not certified easy puzzles.

## Preservation and checks

- Public definitions are exact references/copies of unchanged archived definitions, selected by permanent ID.
- A saved retained hole reopens at its new displayed number with the same progress and results. Newly included previously played holes bring their existing bests with them.
- A withdrawn public hole remains saved and playable in admin; it disappears from public browsing and scoped stats. A withdrawn selected hole safely falls back to the first public hole.
- Every selected hole has a complete safety route independently replayed against the authoritative engine. The twenty included played holes additionally have actual captured player finishes.
- Gameplay, score formulas, thresholds, all archive definitions and IDs are unchanged. No new route search or scoring optimisation was used to choose the mix.
- `selection.json` records exact membership, order, reasons and exported first-finish counts. `validation.json` freezes the archive hash and selected IDs. `scripts/rebalance_public_fifty.mjs` regenerates this view without altering the archive.
