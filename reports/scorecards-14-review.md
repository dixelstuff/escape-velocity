## Route refinement and par

Best finishes across these 12 layouts: two in three strokes, seven in four, three in five. Custom waypoint holes currently default to **par 5**, so the three-stroke MAKEUP → WOMAN → NEWS → automatic SPORT and PASTEL → GREEN → SLIME → automatic COVEN routes are eagles under that default. They are candidates to trial as par 4; one player's best route alone does not establish par. No par or numerical threshold is changed in this update.

NACHO → CHIP → DIP → CLIP → ROPE shortened to NACHO → DIP → CLIP → ROPE, from five to four total strokes. MAP → TRAIN → BED → HOLIDAY → BREAK shortened to MAP → TRAIN → BED → WALL, also from five to four total strokes. These are evidence of exploration followed by refinement, rather than simply guessing until an automatic finish happens. LACE → CLOAK → WITCH → CLOAK → automatic PLAID is a useful real example of valid reuse across legs, which must remain supported.

## Changes

Keep all 11 new waypoint layouts in the player-created bank, including the two unfinished ones as test layouts, bringing that group to 19. Do not claim all are editorially approved; the player's report supplies the gameplay endorsement. Remove only the special backward-boundary exemption for new games. Typed earlier words are scored as ordinary shots, with normal contact and recovery rules. Tapping an earlier lie remains a separate retry action. Reuse in a different leg remains valid, including the LACE/CLOAK example. Existing historical scorecards keep their original backtracking rules for exact replay. Tiny recovery tolerance and numerical contact/green thresholds stay unchanged.
