# Everyday contexts thirty — 6 November 2026

The successful simple word-first approach is retained. This batch emphasises familiar words with several everyday meanings: BAT, PALM, CLUB, BANK, FILE, SEAL, DRAW, BOLT, GROUND, LEAD, CASE, FAN and others. It mixes objects, actions and qualities without quotas or intended solutions.

There are 90 distinct endpoint words, none reused from the current public fifty. Fifty-three have not appeared as tee, dogleg or flag words in any of the prior 600 permanent archive definitions. The other words are older-archive reuse, not a promise that every word is completely new.

Ten ordinary associations per tee were checked independently. Every final triple has at least three legal forward opening hits; restrictive pairings and direct literal-leg shortcuts were replaced. Vocabulary, blocklist, duplicate-triple and existing direct-leg checks passed. No full-route search, score optimisation or intended solution was used. Small setup shots count as opening opportunities; this is a warning check, not a guarantee of enjoyment or completion of every branch.

New holes appear as #1–30 in public and admin. The prior public fifty remain as #31–80. Permanent IDs and settings of all older holes are untouched, so previous routes/bests remain attached to the same holes. The new definitions explicitly use the already-live strong-arrival-v1 rule, including the 0.32 dogleg arrival finish threshold; no gameplay or Cleverness formula changes are made by this batch.

Player calibration retained: BEAM → BALANCE → LIGHT → SOFT at 57 was considered appropriate because LIGHT/SOFT were vague. Do not treat efficient routes receiving low Cleverness as inherently wrong or tune the scorer for particular examples.
