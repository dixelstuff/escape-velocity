# Fifty playful open landscapes — original launch selection

**Historical launch selection.** The current public mix is documented in [the rebalanced public fifty](../public-fifty-rebalanced/README.md). The original fifty remain unchanged in the admin archive.

The public app now browses only this collection, numbered **#1–#50**. Admin (`?admin=1`) retains all **431 playable archive holes**, custom creation and research/export controls. The new holes are #382–#431 there. These are two views of the same permanent IDs and settings, not separately scored duplicates. Switching views preserves stored bests, custom definitions and play history. Public stats and recent finishes show this collection only; admin stats include the whole retained library.

The admin URL is a convenience flag, not authentication. The repository/static files remain public. Public navigation uses its own small `public-manifest.mjs` and does not import the full archive manifest.

## Editorial direction

Keep the successful natural exploration balance, while bringing more humour, movement, texture and surprise into familiar vocabulary: MISCHIEF, WOBBLE, FIZZ, ENCORE, SQUABBLE, GLITTER, RIDDLE, CONFETTI and others. Quirky words are mixed with quieter, flexible targets. No intended solutions were constructed or shipped; nomination files contain triples and independent word contexts.

120 word-first triples were nominated before checks. 81 received deeper actual-landing checks, then fifty were selected. No thresholds, Cleverness formula, automatic finishing rule or saved IDs were changed. All remain mandatory-dogleg Par 5 with unlimited exploration. A bounded checker confirms examples and alternatives; it cannot certify enjoyment or predict human difficulty.

Some rejected alternatives had narrow sampled options (BLUFF → KINDNESS → GLITTER), semantic cycling in the available witness (JUGGLE → PEACE → SHADOW), or less convincing generic chains (NUDGE → GLORY → STREAM). Those are editorial concerns, not new automatic filters. The selected witness is only a mechanical safety example: a longer witness does not prove difficulty, and a short one does not prove triviality. Player feedback remains decisive.

## Validation

- All 50 complete routes replayed through the authoritative engine.
- **571,291** shared-pool outgoing probes replayed from actual reached landings.
- All 410 previous archive definitions preserved byte-for-byte in structured form; retired records remain archived and unavailable.
- Identical public/admin definitions, public-only stats and navigation, shared retained progress, and no public custom creation controls.
- Recent finishes and best-three cards link to results without route expanders.
- Fresh new-best finishes receive a gold trophy glow and NEW BEST! heading. Fewer strokes wins immediately; tied best strokes requires a strictly better unrounded Cleverness score from the same scorer. Returning to a hole or repeating an equal route does not celebrate again.
