# Public favourites and twenty new holes — 13 November 2026

The public list now contains **343 holes**: forty rated favourites at #1–40, 283 retained holes at #41–323, and twenty brand-new holes at #324–343. Seventeen 2/5 holes leave the public view only; their permanent definitions and saved progress remain accessible in the admin archive. Existing average-rated and unreviewed holes remain available. No triple is changed under an existing ID.

## Selection

The decision uses the latest per-hole feedback from six recent cohorts (120 rated holes): everyday transitions, landing opportunities, meaningful discovery, grounded discoveries, landing choice and varied contexts. All eight 5/5 holes are featured. The remaining 32 featured holes are 4/5, prioritising satisfying transitions and positive discovery/exploration notes, with varied familiar contexts. Ratings and notes, rather than Cleverness, strokes or exploration counts, determine selection. This is editorial curation of the available feedback, not a claim to establish each hole's full potential.

PLOT remains featured because its onward discovery redeemed an obvious start. Positive exploration notes support TAP, BLUFF, FORK, SCAN and WAKE. Qualified successes such as RACKET (colour finish), PLUG (obvious music link), CORD (routine finish), SWITCH and DOCK (easy) remain playable after #40. YARD and PANE remain available for continued improvement; the rated 3/5 record is not silently upgraded from score improvement alone. The other retained holes preserve their prior relative order.

Raw play exports and personal journeys are not copied into the repository. `curation.json` records stable IDs, endpoint triples, rating fields and selection decisions for reproducibility.

## New holes at the end

The new twenty follow the current LEVEL_DESIGN brief, giving natural familiar associations priority over conspicuously demonstrating a design trick. Selection mixes multi-use words, actions and qualities with grounded contexts. These are opportunities to playtest, not prescribed routes or guarantees that every finish will satisfy.

Checks are limited to vocabulary/input policy, freshness, duplicate triples, direct literal legs and six independently nominated opening ideas per tee. Restricted candidates were replaced. Setups and immediate arrivals are distinguished in the table; onward possibilities were reviewed editorially, without continuation probes, route searches, simulations or Cleverness optimisation.

Final opening checks: 71/120 forward probes, at least three per hole. The sixty new endpoint words are distinct and have no overlap with the entire previous public collection (including the withdrawn seventeen), latest 100 archive definitions or explicitly compared withdrawn concept-transition batch. Older archive novelty is not claimed.

## Preservation and validation

`validation.json` records the archive prefix hash and appended definitions; its retainedPublicIds is the pre-curation public list. `curation.json` records the final order and public-only withdrawals. Existing definitions, scorer versions, gameplay rules, feedback and durable results are unchanged. Admin preserves its public-first ordering, then the remaining archive, including withdrawn holes.

`tests/test_public_forty_and_new_twenty.mjs` checks curation against ratings, retained ordering, public-only withdrawals, all archived definitions, stable IDs, new-hole positions, opening replay, UK/US freshness and saved-progress retention in isolated stores. Historical batch tests reconstruct their original collections. The browser entry and manifest cache tokens are invalidated.
