# Word-first bank review — 31 October 2026

## What changed

The old guidance actively preferred an authored conceptual journey, a satisfying sense-switch and then a dogleg chosen to support that route. The later requirement for setup shots did not fix that bias. It made a plausible example route stand in for interesting words and an enjoyable landscape.

The new [LEVEL_DESIGN.md](../../LEVEL_DESIGN.md) starts with individually appealing, familiar, versatile words, then checks the combination for natural playability and several possible opening directions. A complete engine-verified route remains a safety check, never the intended solution or evidence of enjoyment. Two-shot approaches, vector distance, novel vocabulary and high Cleverness are not editorial quality gates. No automatic POS/category filter is introduced.

FAME is a good reference: familiar and evocative, with social status, recognition, success, reputation, publicity, identity, achievement and cultural contexts. It is a noun: the goal is to avoid monotonous noun/category corridors, not to reject all noun triples. The same applies to animals and foods—some can offer varied meanings and contexts. An abstract word is not automatically better; vague evaluative words and manufactured noun/verb/adjective templates can also be poor choices.

No new holes or replacement definitions were generated in this task. A recommended replacement would get a new identity if later approved, rather than silently changing a saved hole beneath its existing ID.

## Scope and limitations

I reviewed **all 265 scheduled definitions**: 218 generated, 18 player-authored and 29 user-proposed. The bundled configuration has no additional dogleg triple outside this archive. Private custom holes on a player's device were not available for review.

This is an editorial assessment of the words, their obvious human associations and existing route rationales where available. It is not a claim to have established shortest routes, measured branching diversity, or replayed every possible opening. A short documented route illustrates a concern; it does not establish that the whole hole is easy. A contrived author explanation does not prove players cannot find a good alternative. Existing player enjoyment is evidence worth preserving.

After the seven authorised removals there are **243 playable holes**. All 18 prior retirement combinations remain hidden too (15 shared-archive matches; the others cover saved custom variants). **No shortlisted or watch-list hole was removed.**

First-pass dispositions: 24 priority recommendations, 90 watch-list holes, 129 provisional keeps, 7 newly removed and 15 previously retired. Provisional keep means no strong first-cull recommendation, not a certificate of quality. The watch list deliberately preserves uncertainty rather than proposing wholesale deletion of concrete words.
