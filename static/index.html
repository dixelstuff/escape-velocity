    A POCKET RULEBOOK
    1. Make contact. Your word must connect to the ball word. Longer shots need stronger connections. A miss ends the attempt.
    2. Aim towards the flag. Tiny recovery steps are allowed. You can return to a previously reached word with a connected shot, even backwards. A word too far backward counts as a miss and ends the attempt.
    3. Hole out. Land within chip-in range or on the green. After a short pause, the ball finishes automatically in one more shot. Type your next word in the box; map words are labels.

    Par counts shots in your successful attempt, including the automatic finish. Misses and backward hits restart the attempt. Earlier attempts do not lower Path skill. Unknown words and your current ball word cost nothing.
