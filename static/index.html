    A POCKET RULEBOOK
    1. Make contact. Your word must connect to the ball word. Longer shots need stronger connections. A miss leaves the ball still.
    2. Aim towards the flag. Tiny recovery steps are allowed. You can return to a previously reached word with a connected shot, even backwards. A well-hit shot too far backward splashes into water and returns to your last lie.
    3. Hole out. Reach the green and the ball finishes automatically in one more shot. Play the hole word directly to chip in and save that shot. Once away from the tee, tap the hole word to hit it immediately.

    Each guess counts as one shot, including a miss or water ball, with no extra penalty. The automatic finish from the green also counts as one shot. Unknown words and your current ball word cost nothing. Faint markers show unmade shots ahead of you.
