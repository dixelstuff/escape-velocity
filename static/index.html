      A POCKET RULEBOOK
      1. Make contact. Your word must connect to the ball word. Longer shots need stronger connections. A miss ends the attempt; you can always keep exploring.
      2. Aim towards the flag. Tiny recovery steps are allowed. Tap an earlier word to start another attempt from there. A word too far backward counts as a miss and ends the attempt.
      3. Hole out. Land within chip-in range or on the green. After a short pause, the ball finishes automatically in one more shot. Type your next word in the box; map words are labels.

      Par counts shots in your successful attempt, including the automatic finish. Misses and backward hits end the attempt. Selected clever routes can hole out for eagle. Unknown words and your current ball word cost nothing.
