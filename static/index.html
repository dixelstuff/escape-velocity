    A POCKET RULEBOOK
    1. Make contact. Your word must connect to the ball word. Longer shots need stronger connections. A miss leaves the ball still.
    2. Aim towards the flag. Tiny recovery steps are allowed. A well-hit shot too far backward splashes into water and returns to your last lie.
    3. Reach the green. Finishing gets easier as you approach. You can also play the hole word itself when the connection is strong enough.

    Each guess counts as one shot, including a miss or water ball, with no extra penalty. Unknown words and your current ball word cost nothing. Faint markers show unmade shots ahead of you.
